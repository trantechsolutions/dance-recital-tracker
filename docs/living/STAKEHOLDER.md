# Recital App: What's New

Plain-language notes for families, the studio and the board. Newest first.

## 2026-11-13 — Crash fix and pink live highlights (2355b2e..2063acd)

- **The app opens again.** The previous update stopped the app from loading. That's fixed.
- **Live highlights are now pink**, matching the rest of the app: the act on stage, the bar at the bottom, and the buttons the studio uses to run the show. Your dancer's acts still show their name and a pink stripe, while the act on stage has a full pink border and says "On stage now".

## 2026-11-13 — Show-day redesign (b1a79ff..b1a79ff)

**For families**
- **One clear "now on stage" bar.** While a show is running, a bar at the bottom of the screen shows the act number on stage and tells you what to do: "Ava is on in 5 acts. Head back after act 15." It changes colour when your dancer is on.
- **My night.** A new page that answers one question: when is my dancer on? Once a few acts have been timed, it also says roughly how many minutes away that is.
- **Intermission and end of show.** During the break, the app says so and keeps your place. When the show ends, it says "That's the show".
- **A gentler first visit.** New visitors pick their show, then find their dancer, instead of reading a tour. The app remembers your show next time.
- **One search box.** Find a dancer by first name across every show and tap Follow. If two dancers look the same (for example two "Ava K."), the app tells you to check the act numbers.
- **Clearer words.** You **follow** dancers and **save** acts.
- **Optional extras.** The screen can dim itself during the show, and you can ask for an alert when your dancer is 3 acts away (while the app is open).
- **Easier sign-in.** The family link includes the studio code, so most families only need to sign in.

**For the studio**
- **Running the show is one big button.** A volunteer can move the program along with one hand in the dark, undo a mistake, or use a presentation clicker.
- **Optional listening assist.** The phone can notice when new music starts and offer to move to the next act. It never moves on its own without showing a countdown, and it doesn't record anything.
- **Show runners.** You can let a stage manager or parent volunteer run a show without giving them access to anything else.
- **Fewer surprises in setup.** You get a warning about unsaved changes and about dancers in back-to-back acts who have no time to change.
